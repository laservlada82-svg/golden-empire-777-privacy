About the game

ZOKO is a virtual dragon care game with magical rooms and mini-games. Feed and wash your dragon, let him rest, fly through rings in Sky Arena, follow Star Path and play Catch Stars.

Publisher: VS Studio
Creator: Vladan Stanisavljevic
Android package: rs.zoko.game

These pages cover Android version 0.3.9 (version code 12). The planned target audience is players aged 13 and over, including adults. This statement is not an official content rating or an age-verification feature.

Help & contact

Care and mini-games

Feed opens the kitchen: choose a portion or drag food to ZOKO. In the bathroom, drag soap over him and rinse with the shower. Strength recovers while he sleeps. In Sky Arena, drag your finger to steer. Tap the golden tile in Star Path, or tap stars in Catch Stars.

Backups, photos and leaving the game

Use Settings → Backup to export or import progress. In-game photos are stored in a separate local album and must be exported separately. Back returns from a room or closes a dialog; from the main room it opens an exit confirmation. Export your data before clearing storage or removing the app.

Support

Email laservlada82@gmail.com. Include your phone model, Android version, app version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Android 0.3.9 (12) · rs.zoko.game

This policy describes how ZOKO for Android, published by VS Studio and created by Vladan Stanisavljevic (“we”), handles information.

Current version: No player accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party crash-reporting services or real-money purchases are included. Progress and the in-game album stay on your device. Advertising and purchases are planned for a future version only.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, level, virtual currencies, inventory, records, daily task and reward information, language, sound and other settings in local app storage. Its album stores up to 20 in-game photos. These are images of the game, not photos taken with the phone camera. Local data is used to run the game, resume progress and remember your choices.

2. Network access and sharing

The current Android game does not upload progress, album images or player identifiers to us. It does not include account registration, advertising or analytics services. Game resources are bundled. Although the Android manifest includes Internet permission, its WebView is restricted to the app’s local HTTPS asset origin; that address serves packaged files rather than a public game server. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera or advertising-ID access. We do not maintain a server-side copy of your game progress.

3. Imports and exports

You can choose files through the Android system file picker to import progress or save an exported backup or photo. Exported files exist outside the app’s private storage. If you share a file through another service, that service’s privacy practices apply. Exporting a file is initiated by you; it is not automatic uploading to the publisher.

4. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until changed by gameplay or removed. Delete individual photos in the in-game album. To remove all local app data, open Android Settings → Apps → ZOKO → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the app. Device labels may differ. This removes local progress and the album but does not delete previously exported files; delete those separately in your file manager. Android app backup is disabled in this version. There is no player account or server-side account data to delete.

5. Young players

The planned audience is 13+ and adults. The current game does not ask any player for a name, email address, date of birth or other personal details and does not transmit local gameplay information to us. If you have concerns about a young player’s privacy, contact us. An age label does not replace applicable protections for minors.

6. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send. We use it to respond to and resolve your inquiry and retain it as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the same contact address, subject to applicable retention obligations. This is separate from local gameplay.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, external fonts or forms, and do not set their own cookies.

8. Planned features and policy changes

Before introducing ads, real-money purchases, accounts or other data-processing services, we will update this policy and Google Play declarations to describe the actual SDKs, data, recipients and privacy choices, and implement required consent or disclosures. Mentioning a future feature does not mean it is active now.
