GOLDEN EMPIRE 777

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Current Android version covered: 0.6.3 (version code 10)
Package: com.goldenempire777.prototype

This Privacy Policy explains how GOLDEN EMPIRE 777 for Android (the “App”), developed by Vladan Stanisavljević / VS Studio (“we”), handles information.

Current version: The App works offline. It does not collect or transmit personal data to us or third parties. Game progress and settings stay on your device. Google AdMob is not integrated and no advertisements are displayed. Advertising is planned for a future version only.

1. Information handled by the current App

The App does not require an account or sign-in. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera access. It does not access advertising identifiers, use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, or send gameplay information to a server. The current App does not request Android Internet permission and contains no third-party advertising SDK.

To make the game work and preserve your progress, the App stores the following information locally in its private app storage:

  • Virtual coin balance, jackpot, bet selection, free spins, last win and last game board.
  • The time you last claimed the daily bonus, based on the device clock.
  • Adventure progress, chests, relics and collection rewards.
  • Unlocked and selected backgrounds and your sound setting.

This local information is used to resume gameplay, apply game rules and remember your choices. It is not uploaded or shared by the App. Coins and rewards are virtual; the current App has no real-money purchases, payments or cash withdrawals.

2. Sharing and third-party services

The current App does not send user data to us, advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties. It does not use Google AdMob, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics or a third-party crash-reporting service.

3. Local storage, retention and deletion

Game progress and settings remain in the App’s private storage on your device until they are overwritten during gameplay or you remove them. Android app backup is disabled for the current App.

To delete all locally stored App data, open Android Settings, select Apps → GOLDEN EMPIRE 777 → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage / Clear data. Labels may vary by device. Uninstalling the App also removes its local app data. These actions remove saved progress. The in-game “New start” option resets only part of the game state and does not delete all stored data, including unlocked backgrounds and adventure collectibles.

We do not maintain a server-side player account or a copy of your saved progress, so there is no server-side game data for us to retrieve or delete.

4. Planned advertising — not active in the current version

We plan to add Google AdMob advertising in a future version. AdMob is not installed or active in the version covered by this policy, and it does not receive data from this version.

Before releasing a version that introduces advertising, we will update this policy to describe the SDKs actually included, the data they access or collect, the purposes, recipients, retention information and available privacy choices. We will also update the Google Play Data safety and advertising declarations and implement any required disclosures and consent mechanisms. This future plan does not describe current data collection.

5. Children’s privacy

The current offline App does not collect or transmit personal information from any player, including children. If you have a concern about a child’s privacy in connection with the App, contact us using the address below.
